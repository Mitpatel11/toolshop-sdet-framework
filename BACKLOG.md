# Backlog

Anything discovered mid-work that isn't part of the current day's plan goes here —
not into the day's commit, not silently dropped. Groom this weekly during the
sprint review (see `Roadmap.md` Sprint Map).

Status: `[ ]` Open · `[~]` In progress · `[x]` Done · `[-]` Won't do (with reason)

## Format
```
- [ ] <short title> — discovered Day <N> (<date>)
      Why it matters:
      Effort guess: S/M/L
```
